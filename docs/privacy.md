# Privacy Policy for Unicorn SP

**Last updated:** November 25, 2026

**Unicorn SP** (package name: `com.hoodedunicorn.unicornsp.v2`) is an open-source emulation frontend developed by **David / Hooded Unicorn**. This privacy policy explains how information is handled when you use the Unicorn SP Android application.

## 1. Local ROMs, Save States, and Settings
- **On-device storage only:** ROM files, save states, screenshots, controller mappings, and app preferences that you select or create are stored and processed locally on your device.
- **No developer server uploads:** Unicorn SP does not upload your ROM files, save states, or personal gameplay data to external servers operated by the developer.

## 2. Information We Do and Do Not Collect Directly
- **No accounts or profiles:** You do not need to create an account or sign in to use Unicorn SP, and the app does not directly collect your name, email address, or contact details through gameplay.
- **Permissions:** Any device storage, file-picker, or input/controller permissions you grant are used to load ROMs you choose, read/write save files and emulator settings, and respond to controls.

## 3. Google Play, Bundled Libraries, and Crash Diagnostics
- **Distribution & platform services:** When you install or update Unicorn SP through Google Play, Google Play services may collect installation, licensing, performance, or diagnostic information in accordance with the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy).
- **Emulator libraries:** Unicorn SP uses emulation libraries (such as LibretroDroid and emulator cores) to run supported games locally on your device.

## 4. Children's Privacy
Unicorn SP is not directed specifically at children under the age of 13 (or the applicable digital consent age in your region), and the developer does not knowingly collect personal information directly from children. Parents or guardians who supervise a child's device can manage app access and permissions through device and Google Family Link settings.

## 5. Your Choices and Controls
- You can manage app permissions at any time in your Android **Settings > Apps > Unicorn SP**.
- You can delete local ROMs, save states, and app data at any time using your device's file manager or by clearing app data / uninstalling Unicorn SP.

## 6. Changes to This Privacy Policy
This privacy policy may be updated from time to time as Unicorn SP evolves or platform requirements change. The "Last updated" date at the top indicates when the latest version took effect.

## 7. Contact
If you have questions about this privacy policy or Unicorn SP, please contact the developer via dzdoe12@gmail.com.
